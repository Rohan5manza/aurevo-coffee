It started with a simple belief: coffee should feel bold and unique.

Aurevo is an independent coffee label born from our shared curiosity, creativity, and a love for the small rituals that make a day memorable.

We wanted to create a world around coffee, one where each flavor has its own atmosphere, character, and story. Something expressive, a little unexpected, and unapologetically itself.

How do I order THE WORLD?+

Use the Request a Bag button to open our Google order-interest form once it's live. We'll contact you to confirm your request, pricing, delivery availability and next steps. No payment is collected on this website.

What kind of coffee is THE WORLD?+

Our first flavor is planned as a medium-roast ground coffee with cocoa and hazelnut tasting notes and a smooth finish. Final product specifications will be confirmed when orders open.

Where do you deliver?+

Availability and shipping destinations are still being finalized. We will confirm delivery options individually before accepting an order.

Can I follow the launch?+

Absolutely. Our Instagram is where we'll share updates, behind-the-scenes moments and what comes next for AUREVO. Find the link in the footer once connected.
